Live job search · runs entirely in your browser

The latest jobs, pulled from every open board at once

Type a role, or paste your resume and let it pull the keywords out for you. It queries five open job-board APIs at the same time, merges and de-duplicates the results, scores every posting against your keywords, and puts the strongest matches first — inside whatever freshness window you pick. Plus one-click search links for LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and the rest.

Why aren't LinkedIn and Indeed results listed inline? Those sites forbid automated scraping in their terms of service. So this pulls live postings from boards that publish an open API (Remotive, Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, Jobicy, The Muse) and hands you a pre-filled search URL for everything else — one click, no scraping. Your resume text never leaves the browser; only the short role keyword is ever sent to a job board.
